Frequently asked questions

How often should I have professional hygiene?

Usually every 6–12 months. With braces, smoking, heavy plaque or gum disease the dentist may recommend it more often. This is decided at the examination.

Does cavity treatment hurt?

Treatment is done under local anesthesia. If anything is uncomfortable, say so: we pause and add anesthesia if needed.

What is a rubber dam and why is it used?

It is a thin rubber sheet that isolates the tooth from saliva and moisture. It makes it easier to work neatly and cleanly, and keeps materials off the gums and out of the mouth.

Is whitening suitable for everyone?

No, the method has contraindications. An examination comes first: if needed we treat cavities and do hygiene first, then the dentist decides whether whitening is possible.

How much does treatment cost?

Prices are "from": professional hygiene from 4,500 RUB, cavity treatment from 7,000 RUB, whitening 20,000 RUB. The exact cost and treatment plan are given after the examination, before treatment starts.
